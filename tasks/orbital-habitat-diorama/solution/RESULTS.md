## Known limitations

The wall is a shaped cutaway extrusion with a curved upper reinforcement, rather than a fully cylindrical pressure-shell simulation. Furnishings and family photos are stylized; small handwriting is simplified. The Earth textures are global equirectangular maps, with conventional polar compression; clouds are illustrative rather than meteorological data. The day/night cycle changes the sphere's emissive map and cabin lighting rather than computing a physical solar terminator. The sunlight patch is a shader light effect alongside a shadow-casting spotlight. Floating objects are assigned separated paths rather than a collision solver. No comparison to earlier candidates using different baseline SHAs is claimed.
