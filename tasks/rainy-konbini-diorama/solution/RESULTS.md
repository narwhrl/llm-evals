## Assets and limits

All geometry, seeded material textures and Japanese/English signage are original local procedural assets (MIT). Text is drawn using available system Japanese fonts; no font downloads. Three.js and Vite are MIT. No runtime remote texture, audio, CDN or API.

This is a stylized diorama, not a playable shop simulation. Products are miniature geometric packages rather than branded models. Reflections are five small planar mirrors with ripple overlays; no fluid refraction. Glass runoff is stylized line animation. A roof cutaway keeps dense interior details visible at the default angle. No characters. Some props and signage are simplified at overview distance. GPU performance depends on reflective scene cost; no quantitative score against other baselines is claimed.
