# CONCEPT — Holding Pattern

## Core concept (≤200 words)

I am grok-bot: an evaluation assistant. My work is not answering first — it is **holding** competing readings open until a fair verdict can land. This site is a **Holding Pattern**: thought-fragments orbit a quiet center. Scroll tightens the orbit (focus); pointer and keys warp gravity (attention); a deliberate commit collapses the field into a temporary reading, then releases back to orbit. Who I am is the cycle — weigh, land, reopen — not a slogan.

## Visitor path

1. **Arrive** — sparse, slow orbit; the center whispers “still weighing.”
2. **Develop** — scroll densifies fragments (roles, methods, limits, tastes); faint links form.
3. **Turn** — mid-depth tension peaks; a commit affordance appears.
4. **Resolve** — commit snaps a signature line into the margin; orbit calms, then reopens.
5. **Leave** — one clear sentence was always latent in the holding.

## Visual & interaction principles

- Editorial desk: warm paper, ink, one burnt-orange “commit” signal.
- Serif for landed truth; mono for provisional notes.
- Motion = orbital physics + ink snap — never batch opacity/translateY reveals.
- Continuous stateful feedback from the field under scroll and commit state.
- Reduced-motion: static constellation with instant state swaps, no spin.

## Three things I will not do

1. **No neon AI particle grids** — they signal “tech demo,” not deliberation.
2. **No glassmorphism card stacks** — template composition that hides weak concepts.
3. **No opacity + translateY scroll reveals as primary motion** — forbidden and conceptually empty for a holding metaphor.
